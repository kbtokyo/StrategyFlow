        Opening (5–10 min)
        ☿ Ice Breaker Prompt
        Ask participants: "In one sentence, describe the biggest problem your target customer faces that keeps them up at night." Go around the room. Note divergences — they signal different mental models.
        📋 Set the Stage
        • Explain the Value Proposition Canvas structure
        • Clarify: VP side = what you offer, CS side = who you serve
        • Ground rule: No judgment during ideation, challenge during review
        • Use timed rounds (10–15 min per section)
        During Session
        ⚠ Watch For
        • Feature-first thinking — redirect to customer outcomes
        • Assumption stacking — ask "how do we know this?"
        • Groupthink — use silent writing before discussion
        • HiPPO effect — structure equal voice, time-box input
        🔥 Energy Dip? Try This
        Run a Crazy 8s round: 8 ideas in 8 minutes, individually. Then share only the top 2 each. Instantly reactivates creative energy and surfaces hidden ideas.
        Closing (10–15 min)
        🏁 Closing Protocol
        • Run the Multi-Agent full analysis
        • Ask: "What's the one thing that changed in your thinking?"
        • Identify top 3 assumptions to validate this week
        • Assign ownership of next steps
        • Export report for distribution
        Socratic Question Bank
        ☿ Power Questions
        • What evidence would change your mind about this?
        • Who benefits if this fails?
        • What would a skeptical investor say first?
        • If you removed this from the canvas, what breaks?
        • What are we not saying out loud?
        • What does the customer do instead of using us today?
        Workshop Guide
        How to use the Multi-Agent Strategic Intelligence Canvas
        Step 1 — Select Business Model
        Choose the business model that best matches your idea. This context guides all agents to give model-specific insights. You can also use this for any idea — consultancy, startup, NGO, fintech, etc.
        Step 2 — Fill the Canvas
        Fill in as many sections as you can. Even rough bullet points work. The agents reason about what you write, so quality of input affects quality of insight. Use the Socratic Q's button to get probing questions that help you think deeper about each section.
        Step 3 — Run Multi-Agent Analysis
        Click ⬡ Multi-Agent on any block to watch a real deliberation: Socrates questions it, the Strategist answers those exact questions, and the Devil's Advocate rebuts the Strategist's specific claims — each agent reads what the last one actually said. Use ☿ Socratic Q's for standalone questions. Deploy the full team via Deploy All Agents to run that same chain across the whole canvas, ending with a Synthesis Director that resolves the debate and scores the fit gauges from the actual discussion.
        Agent Roles
        ☿ Socrates — Asks probing questions to challenge assumptions
        ◈ Strategist — Answers Socrates' questions, then frames strategy and fit
        ⚔ Devil's Advocate — Rebuts the Strategist's specific claims, not a strawman
        ◉ Market Intel — Referees the Strategist/Devil's Advocate debate with outside evidence
        $ VC Diligence — Investor-lens review: evidence over assertion, risk-first, fact-based scenario questions
        ⬡ Synthesis — Resolves the actual debate into a plan and scores the fit gauges
